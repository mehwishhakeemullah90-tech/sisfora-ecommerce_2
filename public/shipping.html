Shipping & Delivery

Where we deliver

We deliver to every city and town across Pakistan.

How long it takes

2–4 working days to major cities, 4–7 working days elsewhere.

What it costs

Free on orders over Rs. 14,000. Otherwise a flat Rs. 1,677.

Delivery areas

We ship nationwide across Pakistan, including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta and all other cities and towns served by our courier partners. We do not currently ship outside Pakistan.

Delivery times
DestinationEstimated delivery
Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi)2–4 working days
Other cities and towns4–7 working days

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1 working day. Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Delivery may take a little longer during sales and holiday periods.

Shipping charges
  • Free standard delivery on all orders over Rs. 14,000.
  • Orders below that pay a flat delivery charge of Rs. 1,677, shown in your cart before you check out.
  • Cash on Delivery is available nationwide.
Tracking your order

You'll receive an order confirmation email as soon as your order is placed. You can also check your order status at any time in My Account → Order History.
